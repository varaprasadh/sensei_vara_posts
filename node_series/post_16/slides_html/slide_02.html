RAM eater hiding

Backpressure is what stops your Node process from eating all the RAM when you copy a big file the wrong way.

Your Readable produces data at full disk speed.
Your Writable flushes to a slow network socket.

Gap between them? That gap lives in your heap.

fast Readable →→→ heap buffer → slow Writable

The missing signal: .write() returns false when full.
