## Posting tips

- **First 2 lines matter most** — IG truncates after that. Lead with the hook.
- **Hashtags**: drop them in the first comment instead of caption body for a cleaner look.
- **Emoji rationing**: brand uses very few; the ⬇️ and → calls-to-swipe work hard.
- **Carousel covers** sell the click. If a cover isn't pulling, swap to an alt headline from the per-post reports.
- **Cross-post**: Threads + LinkedIn welcome the same captions, slightly more formal tone on LinkedIn.
